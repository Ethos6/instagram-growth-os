# insta-story

Stories are seen by people who already follow you. Their job is not reach; it is
**trust and conversations**. Measure them by replies and sticker taps, not views.

## A sequence, not a pile

Write stories as 3-7 frame sequences with one purpose each:

| Sequence | Frames | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Behind the work | 3-5 | proof you do the thing: process, mess, result |
| Opinion + poll | 3-4 | a take, then a poll that splits the audience |
| Question box -> answers | 1 + 3-6 | collect questions today, answer tomorrow (content for the week) |
| Mini case | 4-6 | problem, what you did, the number, how to get the same |
| Offer / launch | 3-5 | who it is for, what changes, proof, the one way to get it |
| Reshare with a take | 1-2 | reshare a Reel with one line of *why it matters* |

## Stickers and what they're for

- **Poll / quiz:** cheap engagement that teaches you what your audience thinks.
- **Question box:** the best source of next week's content.
- **Slider:** mood/intensity check; light touch.
- **Link:** one destination; put the reason to tap on the frame itself.
- **DM-me prompt** ("reply GUIDE and I'll send it"): the follower moves first,
  so the conversation is welcome. Hand off to `/insta-dm` for the replies.

## Output format

For each frame: `#`, visual (what's on screen), text overlay (<= 12 words),
sticker (type + exact wording), and spoken line if any. Then a note on which
highlight it belongs to (see `/insta-profile`).

## Rules

- Big text inside the safe area (`igos limits`), high contrast, captions on any
  talking frame.
- One sticker per frame. One ask per sequence.
- Don't promise a DM unless the user will actually send it. Never auto-DM people
  who did not message first.
- Run overlays through `igos humanize`.
