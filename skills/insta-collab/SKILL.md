# insta-collab

## Collab posts (two creators, one post)

A Collab post appears on both profiles and shares one comment thread. Use it
when both audiences gain: one teaches, the other demonstrates; or a swap of
expertise. Plan who films, who invites, the hook (`/insta-reel`) and the single
ask. Draft the invite with `/insta-dm` (type 4).

## Brand deals

### 1. Brief the deal back in writing
- Deliverables (format, count, length), dates, review rounds
- **Usage rights:** organic repost only, or paid ads (whitelisting), for how
  long, which regions. Paid usage is priced separately.
- Exclusivity window and category
- Payment terms (deposit, net days), kill fee
- What the creator will *not* say (claims they can't back)

### 2. Make the content native
Write it like the creator's normal content (brand.md). The product appears in
the story; it is not the whole story. One ask, usually the brand's.

### 3. Disclose, every time
- Turn on Instagram's **Paid partnership** label and tag the brand.
- Also say it in plain words **in the visible part** of the caption or at the
  start of the Reel: "#ad", "Paid partnership with @brand", "Gifted by @brand".
- Gifted and affiliate content needs disclosure too.
- Rules differ by country (e.g. FTC in the US, ASA in the UK, ASCI in India).
  If in doubt, disclose more clearly, not less. This is guidance, not legal
  advice.

```bash
igos caption caption.txt --sponsored    # fails if no visible disclosure
```

### 4. Report back
After 7 days: reach, plays, sends, saves, link taps, and the comment themes.
Use `/insta-audit` numbers. Brands rebook creators who report.

## Never
- Make claims about the product the creator hasn't verified.
- Hide the disclosure below "... more" or in a hashtag pile.
