# 5 contract clauses that saved me $20,000
Swipe before you sign your next client
---
## 1. Deposit up front
30% non-refundable. It books the date, not the draft.
---
## 2. Kill fee
If they cancel after kickoff, 50% is due.
---
## 3. Two revision rounds
A third round is billed hourly.
---
## 4. Invoice on handover
The day files are delivered, not the day feedback ends.
---
## Save this for your next contract
Send it to the freelancer who still works without one.
